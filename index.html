Hetero-Genesis Privacy Policy

Last updated / 更新日期：2026-10-16

Developer: Tianyang Studio
Contact: qianxi36355@gmail.com

Overview

This notice covers the local single-player editions and the Douyin / TapTap Demo mini-games of Hetero-Genesis. The studio does not run a game account or gameplay-data server. Mini-games use their host platform and are not standalone offline APKs.

Advertising is disabled in the current prepared builds. Advertising, if enabled in a later build, requires an updated channel-specific disclosure and a separate choice before any ad request or preload. Continuing past a health or privacy notice is not advertising consent.

Data Collection

Game code does not request your legal name, identity documents, birth date, phone number, profile picture, nickname, friends, location, camera, microphone, photos, Bluetooth, sensors or clipboard permissions. It has no chat, user submissions, in-app purchases or online leaderboard.

Window and system information is used to adapt the display and run the mini-game. The studio does not upload local saves, experiment identifiers or diagnostic logs. Host platforms independently process information needed for their accounts, runtime, security and statutory identity / minor-protection services, under their own policies.

Local Game Data

The game may store gameplay data locally on your device to support normal play, including:

  • settings and preferences;
  • tutorial completion state;
  • progress, unlocks, and in-game records;
  • unfinished run progress used to continue a session.
  • gene points, optional ad reward and frequency records, privacy choices and a locally generated random experiment group.

These records are kept on the device or in the mini-game host's local storage for progress and choices. Clear game data through the host or operating system to remove them; this also loses progress. The studio has no server copy. Uninstallation and host backup behavior depend on the platform; manage platform-held data through its privacy controls.

Children

The current China-facing developer age notice is 16+ for fantasy creature combat and organ damage; it is not identity verification or an official age-rating decision. Minors should use the game with guardian guidance and follow the host's identity and play-time restrictions. The studio does not collect identity documents itself.

Third-Party Services

  • TapTap mini-game: TapTap Unity WebGL SDK 2.0.21, provided by 易玩（上海）网络科技有限公司, for conversion/runtime, host storage and window adaptation. Platform account, identity and play restrictions remain with TapTap. TapTap privacy policy.
  • Douyin mini-game: Douyin TTSDK 6.9.5 and Douyin host storage/window APIs. The sidebar reads entry-source fields only. Account, identity and play restrictions remain with Douyin. Read the current platform policy and manage account rights in Douyin's privacy settings. Official developer platform.
  • Standalone Android APK: no native ad SDK or studio online service is enabled. If launched through TapPlay, TapTap provides the platform account and minor-protection services; a standalone APK does not automatically gain those protections. TapPlay explanation.

The SDK versions above describe the prepared candidates, not proof that a game is live or that ads are approved. No friend/profile permissions are requested by this game's code. Host / SDK behavior must not be interpreted as zero platform processing.

Your Rights and Contact

Email qianxi36355@gmail.com for questions, complaints, or access, correction and deletion of information the studio actually holds. Request emails are used only to resolve the request, then deleted unless retention is legally necessary. We respond within 15 working days after necessary verification. Do not send identity numbers or other sensitive documents unsolicited. Contact the host for platform accounts and platform-held data.

For any later enabled advertising version, you can refuse or withdraw in settings without losing normal free play or base rewards. Withdrawal stops new requests; deletion of information already processed by a platform follows that platform's procedure.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game, store configuration, SDKs, permissions, or platform features change. The latest version will be published on this page.
