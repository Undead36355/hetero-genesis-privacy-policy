# Hetero-Genesis Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Developer: Tianyang Studio  
Contact: qianxi36355@gmail.com

## Overview

`Hetero-Genesis` is designed as a local single-player game. Based on the current release preparation status, the game does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

This policy must be reviewed again before each store release if account login, cloud save, ads, in-app purchases, analytics, crash reporting, online features, or other third-party SDKs are added.

## Data Collection

The game is not intended to collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, payment information, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

The current release plan does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, in-app purchases, account login, multiplayer, chat, user-generated content, or cloud synchronization.

## Local Game Data

The game may store gameplay data locally on your device to support normal play, including:

- settings and preferences;
- tutorial completion state;
- progress, unlocks, and in-game records;
- unfinished run progress used to continue a session.

This local data is used only for gameplay continuity and is not intentionally transmitted by the game. You can remove local game data by using your operating system's app data removal tools or by uninstalling the game, depending on the platform.

## Children

The game is not specifically directed to children. Age rating and target audience settings are handled through the relevant store review processes.

## Third-Party Services

The current release preparation assumes no third-party ads, analytics, account, crash reporting, payment, or cloud service SDK is integrated into the game build. If any such service is added, this policy will be updated before release.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the game, store configuration, SDKs, permissions, or platform features change. The latest version will be published on the public policy page.
